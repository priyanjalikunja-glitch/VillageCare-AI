Everyday Health Awareness

Small healthy habits can make a big difference.

💧

Safe Drinking Water

Use a safe water source, follow local water treatment guidance, and store drinking water in a clean, covered container.

🧼

Hygiene and Sanitation

Wash hands with soap, keep food covered, and maintain clean surroundings to help reduce the spread of infections.

🥗

Healthy Nutrition

Eat a varied diet with vegetables, fruits, pulses, and other locally available nutritious foods. Seek professional advice when needed.

🦟

Mosquito Prevention

Remove stagnant water where mosquitoes breed, use suitable protection, and follow local public health advice.

🩺

Health Checkups

Visit a qualified healthcare professional for recommended checkups, vaccinations, and health concerns.

🤝

Community Wellbeing

Share reliable health information and help community members find appropriate local healthcare services.
